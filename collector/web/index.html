Step 1 of 5

Welcome to Job Collector

Every few hours it checks LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Randstad and Robert Half for jobs that fit you, and emails you only the new ones. Setup takes about three minutes.

Where should your job data live? Your settings and saved jobs go in one folder. A folder in Documents (or iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive) is easy to find and back up.

What job are you looking for?

Separate several with commas, e.g. Project Manager, Scrum Master. A job matches if its title contains one of these.
City and province, e.g. Kitchener ON. Leave blank and choose Remote only for remote jobs anywhere in Canada.
Work model

Salary, keywords and other filters can be added later in Settings.

Where should we email new jobs?

Usually your own Gmail. Job Collector sends from it to the address above.
  1. Open Google app passwords (2-Step Verification must be on).
  2. Name it "Job Collector" and click Create.
  3. Copy the 16 letters here. It's not your normal Gmail password.

How often should it check?

Checks run while this computer is on and awake, even when this page is closed. LinkedIn is checked at most every 4 hours.

Find your first jobs

Run the first check now to see it working. It usually takes 2–5 minutes, and you can leave this page while it runs.
